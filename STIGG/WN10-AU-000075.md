# 🔒 STIG Remediation: WN10-AU-000075

**Title:** The system must be configured to audit Logon/Logoff - Logon successes  
**Severity:** Medium  
**Status:** ✅ Compliant (if properly configured)  
**Audit Status:** Manual Check  
**STIG ID:** WN10-AU-000075  
**Vuln ID:** V-220692  
**SRG ID:** SRG-OS-000032-GPOS-00013  
**CCI:** CCI-000067  

---

## 🔍 Finding

Auditing successful logon events is crucial for detecting unauthorized access attempts, tracking user activity, and correlating actions to specific users. This setting ensures that all successful logon attempts are logged, enabling effective monitoring and forensic investigations.

---

## 🛠️ Manual Configuration

**Open Local Group Policy Editor:**  
- Press `Win + R`, type `gpedit.msc`, and hit Enter  

**Navigate to:**  
`Computer Configuration` →  
`Windows Settings` →  
`Security Settings` →  
`Advanced Audit Policy Configuration` →  
`System Audit Policies` →  
`Logon/Logoff`  

**Configure the following setting:**  
- Find and double-click: **Audit Logon**  
- Check the box for: **Success**  
- Click **Apply** and then **OK**
